Research Design and Data Collection

In Week 4, our group explored how generative AI can help international students overcome language barriers in their studies. We discussed possible data sources, including social media platforms such as Xiaohongshu and Instagram, and considered how to organise the information into different categories.

This activity made me realise that collecting data is not as straightforward as I initially thought. People's experiences with AI can be very different, and it is difficult to represent these experiences through simple categories. I also became more aware of ethical issues when using publicly available online content for research.

Overall, this workshop helped me understand the importance of research design and the limitations of quantitative data.
