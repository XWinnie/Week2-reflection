Week 2 Reflection

By Xiang Wang

Creating Websites

This week's workshop systematically introduced several basic approaches to creating websites: using content management systems like CampusPress, building pages with HTML/CSS, and hosting publicly via GitHub Pages.

Social Shaping of Technology

I did not write code from scratch, but instead used existing templates. This process deepened my understanding of the "social shaping of technology" from Week 1's lecture. Technologies do not emerge in a vacuum; seemingly basic HTML structures and CSS styles contain the assumptions and choices of their human designers. The lowered technical barrier allowed me to focus my energy on content and reflection.

Platform Society

Connecting this to Week 2's discussions on platforms and algorithms, I realized that whether using CampusPress or GitHub, we are all situated within a "platform society." We are accustomed to seamlessly posting content on social media daily, yet rarely consider the infrastructure behind it. Building this website made me realize that seemingly natural digital experiences are supported by nested layers of architecture, protocols, and commercial relations.
